## Platform

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS. Specified by the user in the brief. Mobile-first and weight-conscious: a large share of Angolan traffic is mobile on variable connections. Basic SEO (title/meta/OG) required. Structure must be ready to receive a simple CMS later, for news and photos, once the client supplies them.

Bilingual PT-PT / EN with a header language selector, both languages authored in full (confirmed by the user). PT-PT is the default; EN exists because the company's entire purpose is export.

## Users

Three audiences arrive at royalseed.com, in this order of commercial weight:

1. **International buyers, importers and trading desks** (Europe, Middle East, South Africa) assessing whether an unknown Angolan origin is worth a conversation. They read on desktop, they are sceptical, and they are looking for scale, varietal, timing and seriousness — not brand poetry. They speak English.
2. **Institutional counterparties in Angola** — banks, development finance, agriculture ministry contacts, insurers, potential JV partners. They need the project to look real and governed. They read in Portuguese.
3. **Candidates and local partners in Cuanza Sul and Luanda** — technicians, agronomists, suppliers, and the seasonal workforce's supply chain. They need the address, the phone, and evidence the operation exists.

The site is a credential, not a shop. Nobody buys avocados through this page; they decide whether to email `general@royalseed.com`.

## Product Purpose

Royalseed Agro, Lda develops sustainable avocado production in Angola, building a modern agricultural platform oriented toward export.

Success for the website: an unfamiliar buyer or institution finishes the first viewport knowing the varietal, the scale, and the place; finishes the page believing the operation is technically governed rather than aspirational; and has an obvious way to make contact.

## Positioning

A first-generation, export-scale Hass avocado platform in Cuanza Sul, built from a 2,120 ha land base, with the stated intent to integrate production, logistics and eventual processing under one company.

What a neighbouring company could not truthfully copy: the specific land base and its staged development (2,120 ha held, 500 ha in the first phase), the specific site at Lonhe, Quibala, and the founders' pre-implementation study of the avocado value chain in Angola and in Kenya. The honest competitive position is *early and specific*, not *established and proven*.

## Product Principles

1. **The numbers are the argument.** 2,120 / 500 / 231,000 / 23,000 / 710 are the most credible things this one-year-old company owns. They outrank any illustration on the page.
2. **Early is not a weakness unless it is hidden.** State the phase honestly — land held, first phase planted, production estimated. A buyer who discovers an overstatement stops reading; a buyer who finds an honest one keeps reading.
3. **One product, one place.** Hass, at Lonhe, in Cuanza Sul. Never imply a portfolio of crops or a network of sites.
4. **Every blank stays visibly blank.** Where real material does not exist, mark it. Never fill it with plausible generic content.
5. **It must load on an Angolan mobile connection.** Weight is a design constraint, not an optimisation afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Portuguese (PT-PT) is the primary language; English is a full peer, not a machine afterthought. Mobile-first. Contrast must hold for the dark-green-on-warm-white body text and, critically, for gold on any ground — gold is decorative and must never carry small body text on light grounds.
